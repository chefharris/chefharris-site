## Kit free plan — what it could not do, and what was done instead

- Kit's free plan has tags, segments and custom fields; nothing here needs a paid plan. Two things cannot be done from this side: a custom field must exist in the account before Kit stores it, and a tag can only be applied by its numeric id. Both are two-minute clicks for Harris (above). Until the tag id is pasted, the checkbox is visible but its value is not sent.
- "Event on successful submit": the form posts straight to Kit and the browser lands on Kit's own confirmation page, so the site never sees Kit's answer. The GoatCounter event is sent as the form is submitted (after the browser has checked the email field). It counts submits; confirmed subscribers are counted in Kit. Doing it "properly" would need an in-page success message, which has no signed text.
